Markets are rarely confusing because there is not enough information. They are confusing because too much information arrives at once, without a clear way to separate signal from noise.

A good research tool should work more like a judgment support system than a dashboard. It should decide what the user needs to see first, which changes are worth comparing, and which signals deserve another look.

## Questions worth asking

- What changed?
- Where did the change come from?
- Is the signal repeatable or just event-driven?
- Under which conditions would the same judgment be wrong?

The difference between a tool that shows data and a tool that helps decisions usually lives in this layer.
